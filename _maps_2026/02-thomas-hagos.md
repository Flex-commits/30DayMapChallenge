Where can someone go for help in NYC, and how close is it to the train?

I mapped 21 publicly listed homelessness service access points and connected each address to its nearest subway or Staten Island Railway station. The lines show straight-line proximity, not walking routes. The interactive map also includes a points view, service filters, and links to official information.

[Explore the map](https://flex-commits.github.io/30-days-mapping/finding-support/) · [Read the project](https://flex-commits.github.io/MUSA-Portfolio/projects/finding-support/)

![Finding Support: NYC homelessness services connected to nearby rail stations](https://flex-commits.github.io/30-days-mapping/finding-support/finding-support-poster.png)

Sources: NYC311, ACCESS NYC, NYC DYCD, MTA stations, U.S. Census address matching, NYC Department of City Planning, and Natural Earth. Snapshot checked November 2, 2026. This is a public access-point directory, not all shelters or live bed availability. Distances do not account for bus and ferry connections, street routes, entrances or step-free access.

Made with Python and Leaflet. Research, code and editing were assisted by AI. The source repository includes the interactive code and a downloadable bundle of analysis scripts and saved inputs.
